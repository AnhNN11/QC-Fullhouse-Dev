## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a NEW distinctly different silhouette for the DolphinX cyber dolphin logo. Do NOT reproduce the long diagonal leaping dolphin used in previous concepts.
Single compact emblem on a genuinely transparent square canvas. A sleek dolphin curls into a near-circular C-shaped silhouette: head positioned in upper right, short recognizable dolphin beak pointing slightly upward, curved back sweeps across the top and down the left, body tapers around the bottom, split tail bends inward toward the center. Large deliberate central negative space creates a subtle coding bracket shape. Strong compact circular flow without any enclosing circle, shield, or badge border. Clearly a dolphin, not a shark, snake, dragon or fish.
Style: premium futuristic software brand, bold flat vector-like geometric shapes, few elegant angular facets, narrow bright cyan visor eye, confident and cool. Three or four large interlocking planes rather than many small panels. One minimal circuit line integrated into a fin, no decorative wires everywhere. Palette primarily midnight navy #071C3C with electric blue #087DDD and cyan #15D9F2 accents. Avoid large white belly. Shape must remain recognizable at icon sizes.
Optically centered, generous transparent margin, entire tail and snout visible. No text, no lettering, no wordmark, no mockup, no gradient, no shadow, no glow haze, no 3D chrome, no checkerboard, no watermark, no cute cartoon smile. The pose and composition must be new: coiled compact C-shaped dolphin with upward-facing head, NOT a diagonal leaping dolphin.
