# Friendly technology whale for children

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create an original premium friendly tech WHALE logo for a coding learning platform for CHILDREN aged roughly 7–14. The previous direction was too aggressive; this new logo must feel welcoming, curious, clever and fun. Icon only on a genuinely transparent background with actual alpha.
A compact chubby little whale in a three-quarter side view facing right, rounded broad head, short rounded flippers, small expressive tail raised behind. Clearly a whale, NOT a dolphin: broad short muzzle, no long pointed beak. Two warm bright expressive eyes and a small relaxed curved smile. No teeth, no angry eyebrows, no sharp visor, no armor. Lively buoyant swimming pose, balanced centered composition.
A simple original silhouette, 4–6 large clean color shapes, rounded geometry, crisp vector-style edges, polished children's educational brand quality. Subtle technology cue: just ONE small cyan circuit trace with two rounded nodes on the side, or a simple code bracket motif naturally integrated into a rounded flipper. Keep the technology detail secondary to the lovable whale.
Palette: cheerful medium blue body, deep navy small outline/details, cyan highlights, pale blue belly, small white eye highlights. Use solid flat fills, no gradients, no glow, no 3D. Blue should dominate, not dark navy. Recognizable at small app icon size. Not a sticker with a white border.
Only one whale. Generous transparent margins, all flippers and tail visible. No surrounding decorations, no scene, no shield, no circle badge, no wordmark, no text, no letters, no watermark, no printed checkerboard. Friendly and age-appropriate without looking like an infant toy. Simple, warm, intelligent, memorable.
